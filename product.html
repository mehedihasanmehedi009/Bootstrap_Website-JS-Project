A lush, tropical classic with iconic split leaves. The Monstera brings a sculptural feel to your favorite bright corner—and grows more character with every new leaf.

1

Carefully packed. Delivery estimates shown at checkout.

Place in bright, indirect light. Water when the top 2 inches of soil feel dry, and always let excess water drain. Wipe leaves gently to keep them dust-free.

Monstera is toxic if chewed by cats or dogs and may irritate the mouth. Keep it out of reach; choose a pet-friendlier option if curious pets share your home.

Repot every 1–2 years or when roots circle the pot, ideally in spring. Move up just one pot size and use a well-draining mix.

Plant parent tips

01 / WATERING

Check the soil, not the calendar.

Feel the top layer before watering. Plants respond to their environment, not your reminder app.

02 / LIGHT

Follow the light through your home.

Notice where the sun lands across the day, then choose a plant that enjoys that spot.
